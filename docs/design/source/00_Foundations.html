Nudge
Design system 1.0 · iOS & Android

The business remembers everything, so you don’t have to.

An AI business memory for teams of one to ten. Every screen answers three questions — what is important, what should I do, and why should I care — and removes anything that doesn’t.

Nudge is organised around
People→Context→Promises→Actions
Not a CRM organised around
Leads→Deals→Pipelines→Reports
